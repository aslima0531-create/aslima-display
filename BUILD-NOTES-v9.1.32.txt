ASLIMA V9.1.32 — Expandable Prayer Warning Banner

Changes
- Replaced the permanently expanded guidance card with a compact tap-to-expand banner.
- Collapsed view shows a short warning/guidance title, a short remaining-time badge, and a tap prompt.
- Expanded view shows the complete guidance text without ellipsis, line clamping, or text truncation.
- Tap the banner again, tap outside it, or press Escape to collapse.
- Banner remains aligned to the prayer panel.
- Added short-screen protection: the small ASLIMA mark temporarily hides only while an expanded card would overlap it, then returns when collapsed.
- Preserved the existing Hanafi timing windows and obligatory-prayer exceptions.
