ASLIMA v9.1.28 — Compact prayer warning banners

Changes:
- Added a compact centered warning banner that does not cover the full tablet screen.
- Amber conditional guidance appears during the after-Fajr and after-Asr periods.
- Red warnings appear during sunrise, the five-minute zawal window before Dhuhr, and the final fifteen minutes before Maghrib.
- Red warning windows take priority over amber guidance.
- Fajr and Asr obligatory-prayer exceptions are included in the displayed wording.
- Banner is pointer-events:none, so it cannot block any tablet controls.
- Banner automatically hides while the Azaan overlay or Timing Source modal is open.
- Invalid or chronologically impossible timing data fails safely with no banner.
- Added the warning module to the service-worker offline cache and bumped the cache/version query to v928.

Preserved:
- Exact per-recording Azaan phrase cue synchronization from v9.1.25.
- All five local Azaan MP3 files and their checksums.
- Firebase phone remote, manual timing editor, VRIC/location timing modes, drawer controls, Hijri calendar handling, and background image.
