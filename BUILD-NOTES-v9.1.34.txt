ASLIMA V9.1.34

Added Tahajjud and Duha / Chasht supplementary prayer visuals.
Added automatic gentle beep reminders at the start of each supplementary prayer window.
These reminders do not use the Azaan overlay or Azaan audio recordings.
Tahajjud is shown as the last third of the night.
Duha / Chasht is shown from 20 minutes after sunrise until 10 minutes before Dhuhr.
