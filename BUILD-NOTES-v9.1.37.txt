ASLIMA V9.1.37

Implemented the cleaner bottom-area redesign.
- Replaced the always-expanded three-card supplementary section with one contextual supplementary-prayer card.
- The card now shows only the active supplementary prayer or the next upcoming one.
- Added a View All button that expands a compact list of Tahajjud, Ishraq, and Duha/Chasht.
- Replaced the always-visible Jumu’ah bar with a small expandable Jumu’ah Schedule pill.
- Jumu’ah is now accessible any day by tapping the pill.
- On Fridays, the Jumu’ah panel auto-expands by default and can still be collapsed.
- Reduced lower-screen crowding and separated the Jumu’ah UI from the supplementary-prayer UI.
- Preserved all five existing Azaan recordings and all supplementary-prayer beep-only behavior.
